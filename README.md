## Complete Core Electrical Engineering Coursework/Electives

Understanding circuit theory and digital systems design provides the necessary background skills required to design PCB hardware safely and efficiently.


Complete electrical engineering electives focusing on circuit design, signal integrity, and power delivery preferrably before your senior year. Here are some UWB courses that you should take.

* B EE 331 & 332 (Devices and Circuits).
* B EE 271 ( Digital Cicuits and Systems)
* B EE 361 (Applied Electromagnetics)

## Learn PCB Designing Software

Transitioning academic circuit concepts into real-world hardware requires hands-on skills with Electronic Design Automation (EDA) software.

Dedicate free-time to building projects in hardware design, using GitHub to track your schematic revisions and layout files. Complete training courses and target certifications for platforms like Altium or KiCad to prove your technical skills to recruiters. 

Here are some free PCB design training courses:
https://learntube.ai/learn/course/pcb-design-using-kicad
